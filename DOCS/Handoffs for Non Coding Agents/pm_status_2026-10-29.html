ThrottleIQ: where we are and what to do next

Product manager view · 29 Oct 2026 · Budget: $0, free tiers only · Sources: HANDOFF_Document.md, needs_attention.md, issues_open.md, judge.md, git log

In short: the product is far ahead of its users. October had 441 commits and about 2,300 passing tests, but the last build on Google Play is from 29 August. Builds +32, +33 and +34 were made and never uploaded, and almost nothing on main has run on a real phone.

What to do: stop adding features for two weeks. Test on a phone, ship one build to a Play closed test, and get 10–15 real riders onto it. The free Firebase plan (Spark) fits a beta that size. Leave everything that costs money for after riders show they come back.

2. What "free tiers only" means here

Free, use it

  • Firebase Spark: Firestore (50k reads / 20k writes a day), Auth, Hosting, Analytics, Crashlytics
  • App Check with Play Integrity (free in normal volume)
  • Google Play: the developer account is already paid for
  • Cloudinary free tier (add a size cap); Thunderforest tiles, 150k a month
  • GitHub Actions, CodeQL, secret scanning, Dependabot
  • Kaggle / Colab free GPUs if Indriyo training restarts

Costs money, defer

  • Blaze: $0 at today's use, but it needs a card and has no hard spending cap. Wait for a trigger (below).
  • Apple Developer ($99/yr): iOS stays on the founder's phone
  • Lawyer (ToS, PDPO, CLA): hold the contributor announcement until then
  • Apify (up to $10/mo): turn it off; Facebook research is done
  • Contributor T-shirts (costing draft): park
  • SMS provider for crash alerts: not before calibration anyway

Move to Blaze when one of these happens: daily riders pass about 15 (Spark's ceiling is close), deletion requests become regular, or the Play / App Store review requires in-app account deletion that runs on the server.
