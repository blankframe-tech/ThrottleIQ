The problem

Riders fly blind.

Cars have had onboard computers for a decade. Motorcycles still don't — and the ride-tracking apps built for cyclists and runners don't understand motorcycle-specific risk or motorcycle-specific upkeep.

📓
No black box Speed, braking, and route data disappear the moment the ride ends — nothing to review, nothing to learn from.
🛠️
Maintenance by memory Chain, oil, and tires are tracked in a notebook or not at all, instead of against the distance actually ridden.
🚨
No safety net If a rider goes down alone on a back road, nobody knows until they're overdue.
The solution

Everything a rider needs, one app.

Offline-first ride tracking, safety, maintenance and community — built rider-first, not adapted from a running app.

🛣️

Ride Recording

  • Background tracking — keeps recording screen-locked or backgrounded
  • Live speed, acceleration, jerk, altitude, distance
  • Smart alerts: overspeed, hard braking, rapid accel, fatigue past 90 min
📊

Ride Analysis

  • Per-ride summary: max speed, distance, duration, event counts
  • Separates moving time from stops automatically
  • Export any ride as JSON or GPX
🆘

Crash Detection & Emergency

  • Detects impact + speed-drop signature, starts a 60s cancel countdown
  • Live, token-based location link — contacts need no account
  • Up to 5 emergency contacts, revocable anytime
🏍️

Garage & Maintenance

  • Fleet view of every bike, its stats, and service history
  • Service intervals tracked against actual distance ridden
  • 13 service types, plus a custom type for anything else
📍

Rider Places

  • Fuel, garages, and parts shops — verified and user-contributed
  • Ratings and reviews with photos
  • One-tap nearest fuel stop while riding
👥

Social & Community NEW

  • Ride feed, forums, and per-ride audience controls
  • Group rides: invite up to 10 friends onto one shared live map
  • Save any ride as a reusable route with turn-by-turn guidance
Under the hood

Offline-first, syncs when it can.

Recording never depends on a signal. Everything lands in local SQLite first; the cloud catches up when it's available.

On the bike — Flutter app

  • Local SQLite is the source of truth, works with zero connectivity
  • Riverpod state — ride recording, live sync status
  • Material 3 UI + Flutter Map for routes and live tracking

In the cloud — Firebase

  • Firestore: rides, bikes, maintenance, POIs, reviews
  • Storage for photos, Auth for email + Google sign-in
  • Cloud Functions for crash notifications and exports
record → local SQLite → marked unsynced → auto-sync on resume & every 5 min → Firestore
Safety & privacy

Your ride, your data.

Privacy isn't a settings toggle bolted on afterward — it's enforced where it matters: the database rules, not just the UI.

🔒

User-owned data

Every rider's data lives under their own account; Firestore rules enforce user-only access, and deletes are permanent on request.

🏠

Privacy zones

Shared rides auto-clip the first and last 200m of the route — home location is never exposed.

⏱️

Time-limited sharing

Live emergency links use an unguessable token and expire after 24 hours, and can be revoked anytime.

🙈

No ad tracking

No tracking cookies, no third-party analytics queried. Secrets and keys are never committed to the repo.
