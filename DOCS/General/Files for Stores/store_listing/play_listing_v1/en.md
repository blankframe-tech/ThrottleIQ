## Full description (≤ 4,000)

<!-- full -->
ThrottleIQ is a service book for your motorcycle that goes with you to any garage, and a ride recorder that keeps working where your signal doesn't. Made in Bangladesh, in English and Bangla.

ONE SERVICE BOOK FOR ANY BIKE
• Add your bike and its odometer; ThrottleIQ shows what it needs next and roughly when, by kilometres or by date, whichever comes first
• Service schedules for popular models, adjusted for traffic and dusty or wet roads
• Log a whole service visit at once, at any garage, with the bill and a photo of the receipt
• Papers: reminders 30 days before your tax token, insurance, fitness or registration run out
• Fuel log with km/L between full-tank fills, and running costs per km
• Export a service record PDF when you sell the bike, so the history goes with it

RIDES THAT RECORD WITH NO SIGNAL
• Every ride is saved on your phone first and keeps recording through dead zones and with the screen locked
• Distance, moving time versus stops, speed and route for each ride, with an animated replay on the map
• Import or export rides as GPX
• A Year in Review story you can share at the end of the year

IN BANGLA
• The whole app works in Bangla or English, with numbers and units the way you read them
• Home-screen widgets in your language

LIVE SHARE FOR FAMILY
• Before a long ride, send a live link: family or friends see your location, speed and battery in any browser, no app needed
• You start it, you can stop it any time, and the link stops working after 24 hours

RIDER PLACES AND FORUMS
• Find fuel stops, garages and parts shops near you, added and rated by riders
• Brand and model forums to ask owners of the same bike
• Follow riders and share rides; you choose who sees each ride, and the start and end of every shared route are trimmed so your home stays private

BACKUP
• Bikes, service logs and rides back up to your account when you're online
• Sign in with Google or email

ThrottleIQ is free. It's built by a rider and updated often; tell us what's missing from the app's bug-report form.
<!-- /full -->
