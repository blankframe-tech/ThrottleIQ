# Closed-tester onboarding message

Send this the moment someone is added to the Play Console internal-testing
tester list (`NEEDS_YOUR_ATTENTION.md` #1) or handed the GitHub APK
directly. Recruitment templates (`outreach_templates.md` #1) get someone to
say yes; this is what happens right after, and it's the step most likely
to get skipped under "I'll just add the email and move on" — a tester who
gets added and never onboarded is a silent churn, not a real test.

---

**EN:**
> You're in — thanks for testing ThrottleIQ.
>
> **To install:** [if Play Console] check your email for the opt-in link
> from Google Play, tap it, then open the Play Store app and search
> ThrottleIQ (or use the direct link I'll send). [if GitHub APK] download
> the APK from [link] — Android will warn about "unknown sources," that's
> expected for a beta not on Play yet, allow it just for this install.
>
> **What I actually want from you, specifically:**
> 1. Record at least one real ride — does it survive a signal dead zone
>    without losing data?
> 2. If you hit a pothole, hard brake, or sudden stop — did it ever
>    falsely think that was a crash? This is the single most important
>    thing to tell me, good or bad.
> 3. Anything that felt broken, confusing, or just annoying — big or
>    small, say it.
>
> Reply here or DM me directly, whichever's easier. No formal bug-report
> format needed — "the maintenance screen did something weird" is a
> perfectly good report.
