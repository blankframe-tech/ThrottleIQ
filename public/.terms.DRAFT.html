4. Safety: read this before you ride

ThrottleIQ is not a safety device and not an emergency service

  • Do not use the app while riding. Set it up, start recording and send any live-share link before you set off, or stop safely first. Keep your eyes on the road. Obey traffic law, wear a helmet and ride within your ability.
  • Crash detection does not alert anyone. It is being tested. It only records possible impacts and asks you about them after the ride. There is no alarm, no countdown and no message to you, your emergency contacts or the emergency services.
  • Emergency contacts are never contacted. The app stores the contacts you type in, but sends them nothing.
  • The app does not call for help. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999 (Bangladesh) or your local emergency number.
  • Do not rely on ThrottleIQ as a safety net. GPS, sensors, batteries, networks and phones fail. A live-share link stops updating when your phone dies or loses signal.

Speed figures, riding scores, hard-braking and acceleration counts, lean-angle and g estimates, maintenance forecasts, ride-cost figures and "faster than usual" notes are estimates from phone sensors and your own entries. They can be wrong. Do not use them to decide whether your motorcycle is safe to ride, or as evidence of how anyone rode.

Route guidance is geometric only: no street names, no lane guidance and no rerouting. It does not know about closures, traffic or hazards. Places, cop checkpoints, AI-camera locations and opening hours come from other riders and from OpenStreetMap; they may be out of date or wrong. Always follow road signs and the law.

8. Reporting, blocking and how moderation works

Report

  • A chat message: press and hold the message, then tap Report.
  • A rider: open their profile (in a chat, tap their name at the top), tap ⋮, then Report User.
  • A forum post or a shared ride: tap ⋮ on it, then Report.

The rider you report is not told who reported them. A report on a message or forum post keeps a copy of that content as evidence, readable only by the reviewer, and deleted 90 days after the report is reviewed.

Block

Open the rider's profile, tap ⋮, then Block User. A blocked rider cannot message you, follow you or see your followers-only rides, and blocking removes follows both ways. You can unblock in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Blocked users. Blocking does not send a report; do both if the rider broke the rules.

Review and action

  • A person reads every report within 24 hours of receiving it.
  • To review a reported chat message, the reviewer reads that message and the messages around it, and nothing else in the conversation. Conversations nobody reported are not read.
  • Depending on how serious it is and whether it happened before, the outcome is one or more of: no action, a warning by email, removal of the content, a suspension (usually 7 days, during which the account cannot sign in), or a ban (the account is closed for good).
  • Sexual content involving a minor is reported to the authorities.

Appeals

Email hello@blankframe.tech from the address you signed up with to ask about a report or appeal an action. Appeals are answered within 7 days.
