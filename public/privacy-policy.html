Throttle IQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Throttle IQ ("the app", "we", "us") is a motorcycle ride-tracking and rider-community app published by Blankframe.tech. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. Information we collect

DataWhat it's for
Precise location (GPS), including in the background while a ride is being recordedTo record your ride's route, speed, distance, and to detect possible crashes. Background access is required so recording continues if your screen locks or you switch apps mid-ride.
Motion sensor data (accelerometer)To measure acceleration, hard braking, and to help detect a possible crash.
Account info (email address, display name, profile photo)To create and manage your account, and to identify you to other riders if you use social features.
Ride data you create (routes, stats, notes, photos)To show your ride history, and — if you choose to share a ride — to display it to the audience you select.
Bike and maintenance records you enterTo track your garage and service history.
Emergency contact details you enter (name, phone/email)So you can share a live ride-tracking link with people you choose during a ride.
Photos you upload (e.g. for place reviews or your profile)Stored via Cloudinary to display within the app.
Basic device and diagnostic infoProvided by Firebase (Google) to keep the app running and to diagnose crashes.

2. How your data is shared

  • With other riders: if you use social features (following, sharing a ride, posting in forums, reviewing a place), the content and audience you choose determines who can see it. You control this per ride via audience settings.
  • With emergency contacts: if you start a ride, a live-tracking link can be shared with contacts you add. Anyone with that link can see your live location, speed, and battery level until the ride ends or the link expires.
  • With service providers: your data is processed by Google Firebase (authentication, database, backend logic) and Cloudinary (photo storage) strictly to operate the app. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.
  • Privacy zones: when you share a ride publicly or with followers, the first and last ~200 meters of the route are automatically trimmed so your home location isn't exposed.

3. Your choices

  • Location permission (including background) can be managed anytime in your device's app settings. Denying it will prevent ride recording from working.
  • You can delete individual rides, remove emergency contacts, or change ride-sharing audience at any time in the app.
  • To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below — we will delete your account data from our systems except where we're required to retain it by law.

4. Data retention

We keep your account and ride data for as long as your account is active, or until you request deletion. Live-share links expire automatically once a ride ends.

5. Children's privacy

Throttle IQ is not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law).

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices (including Firebase Authentication and access-controlled databases) to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
