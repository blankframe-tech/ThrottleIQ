## What NOT to do in this window

- No paid installs ads — `marketing.md` §8 holds paid acquisition until
  Phase 2-3, after organic retention is confirmed healthy. Don't reach for
  a boosted post even if organic reach feels slow in week 1-2; low-volume
  organic is expected and noisy, not a sign the channel failed.
- No mass-identical posting across many groups in the same week — this
  reads as spam in close-knit BD moto communities per `marketing.md` §5;
  stagger by a few days and adapt the opening line per group.
- No iOS-specific push in this window — TestFlight is a Phase 2 channel,
  not Phase 0/1 (`marketing.md` §5.3).
