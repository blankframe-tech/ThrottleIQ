# iDEA Project (ICT Division, Bangladesh) Pitching Framework

This skill guides founders and agents in preparing compliant, winning pitch decks and video scripts for the **iDEA Project** under the ICT Division, Government of Bangladesh.

---

## 1. iDEA Evaluation Criteria & Grant Structure

The Innovation Design and Entrepreneurship Academy (iDEA) evaluates startups based on four core pillars:
1. **Problem-Solution & Innovation Fit (25%)**: Is the problem real, acute, and prevalent in Bangladesh? Does the technology provide an innovative, scalable answer?
2. **Market Potential & Alignment with National Goals (25%)**: Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM), impact on 'Smart Bangladesh', road safety, civic welfare, or digital inclusion.
3. **Execution Capability & Technical Rigor (25%)**: Working prototype or live product, architecture robustness, offline resilience for local conditions, intellectual property / defensibility.
4. **Business Viability & Financial Sustainability (25%)**: Clear monetization paths, unit economics suited to Bangladesh's purchasing power, transparent grant utilization (typically BDT 10 Lakh for pre-seed).

---

## 2. Part 1 — Slide Deck Specification (9-Slide Rule)

Every pitch deck prepared for iDEA must follow the exact 9-slide template:
1. **Introduction – Company**: Name, logo, clear tagline, contact info, vision statement.
2. **Problem & Solution Scenario**: Current pain points in Bangladesh; direct, clear product answer.
3. **Market Size / Possibilities**: Quantified TAM, SAM, and SOM in Bangladesh Context (and regional expansion potential).
4. **Competitive Advantage / Unique Features**: Status quo vs existing alternatives (global & local), core technological moats.
5. **What is your Need?**: Financial ask (e.g. 10 Lakh BDT Pre-Seed Grant), allocation breakdown, operational & mentorship needs.
6. **Technology Strategy**: High-level and detailed architecture, mobile/cloud components, data privacy, edge/offline processing.
7. **Business Strategy**: Go-To-Market (GTM) plan, customer acquisition channels, partnership roadmaps (B2C & B2B).
8. **Financial Strategy**: Pricing model (BDT), revenue streams, 3-year P&L forecasts, break-even analysis.
9. **Management Team**: Key team members, technical credentials, domain expertise, advisors.

---

## 3. Part 2 — 5-Minute Video Pitch Rules

- **Total Duration**: Strictly 5 minutes (300 seconds). Exceeding 5 minutes causes disqualification or scoring penalties.
- **Section Breakdown**:
  - **Business Plan**: Exactly 3 minutes (180s)
  - **Technical Architecture**: Exactly 1 minute (60s)
  - **Management Team**: Exactly 1 minute (60s)
- **Delivery Rules**:
  - Never read verbatim from slides. Speak conversationally and directly to the camera.
  - Involve all core team members.
  - Video resolution: 1080p, format: MP4 or AVI.
  - Demonstrate working software/app on a real device.
